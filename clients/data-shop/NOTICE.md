# Notices

The shop file in this package is built by Wenmar Open (https://github.com/wenmar-pro/wenmar-open, MIT) from these sources.

| Dataset | Source | Licence | Attribution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vehicle recall campaigns, United States (`recalls_us`) | NHTSA recall flat files, https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/ffdd/rcl/ | Public domain (United States government work) | None required. |
| Vehicle recall campaigns, Canada (`recalls_ca`) | Transport Canada, Vehicle Recalls Database, https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/1ec92326-47ef-4110-b7ca-959fab03f96d | Open Government Licence - Canada | Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence - Canada. |

The vehicle names the campaigns are matched to come from NHTSA's vPIC, through the Wenmar Open data file.

A recall campaign lists a year, a make and a model. Nothing in the file says a recall applies to one vehicle.
